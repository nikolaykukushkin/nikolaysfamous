World Cup of Beer 2026

Sixteen lagers, four groups, one summer — tasted blind while the real tournament runs. Every beer is a nation actually playing in 2026.

Groups & bracket Rubric & score sheet Printable cards
How it works. One group per night. Katy pours each beer blind into a numbered cup — A, B, C, D — and keeps the key. Everyone tastes, fills a score sheet, and force-ranks the four 1–4. Ranks convert to points (1st = 4, 2nd = 3, 3rd = 2, 4th = 1) and are summed across tasters; the rubric total breaks ties. Top two from each group advance to an eight-beer knockout: quarter-finals, semis, final. Grouping is by style, so within a night you’re comparing like with like.

The knockout

Top two from each group make the final eight. Quarter-finals cross the groups so no two group-mates meet again before the semis:

  1. QF1: Winner Group 1  v  Runner-up Group 2
  2. QF2: Winner Group 2  v  Runner-up Group 1
  3. QF3: Winner Group 3  v  Runner-up Group 4
  4. QF4: Winner Group 4  v  Runner-up Group 3

Semis: QF1×QF3 and QF2×QF4. Then the final — one last blind pour, winner take all.

Sixteen nations, all confirmed in the live 2026 field (12 groups, 48 teams). “La Tuya” is a contract beer labelled as brewed in Portugal, so it flies the Portuguese flag here.
Group assignments are by tasting style, not the real draw — the real group each nation landed in is noted on its card. Tonight: Groups 1 & 2.
